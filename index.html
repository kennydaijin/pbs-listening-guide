English Summary

President Trump and Defense Secretary Hegseth downplayed the reports, while a senator and a retired admiral raised serious concerns about supply logistics in the region. A former Navy chief of operations explained that regional ports are no longer available for resupply because of instability tied to Iran.

5. Ebola Outbreak

コンゴ民主共和国で拡大するエボラ出血熱の流行を特集。確認感染者数は4500人超、死者は2100人以上に達し、過去の流行より速いペースで死者数が増えています。WHO事務局長テドロス氏の発言や、現地の医療従事者・市場関係者の声、UCLAの疫学者Anne Rimoin氏へのインタビューを通じて、対応の難しさ(医療従事者の未払い賃金、診断キットの不備、紛争による医療アクセスの制限など)が語られます。

English Summary

The Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of Congo is now the second-largest on record, driven by the rare Bundibugyo strain, for which no vaccine exists. Healthcare workers, some unpaid for months, are striking even as cases surge, and ongoing conflict over minerals is blocking access to care. Epidemiologist Anne Rimoin explains that diagnostic delays and weak infrastructure allowed the outbreak to spiral out of control before it was even officially declared.
